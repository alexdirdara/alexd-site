01

Multiple Faces, One Experience

What’s the difference between the “amazing day” you and the “other days” you?

Your expertise, competence and ability to help are there on both kinds of days.

Sleep, diet and exercise all matter, but you’ve likely had a sleep-deprived “amazing day” and a well-rested “other day.”

The difference is the state you’re in. People talk about states loosely, like “the state of a winner” or “get excited to be in a better state.”

We mean actual changes in how your brain and nervous system are operating. Each state comes with its own version of you.

Each state has its own

  • Self-imageHow confident and worthy you feel
  • GoalsWhat you’re trying to do right now
  • AttentionWhat you notice around you, and in people
  • MoodYour emotions, and how your body feels
  • MemoryWhich memories come to mind most easily
1 Truth 1

You’re constantly shifting through different states, without realizing it.

Your “amazing day” and your “other days” come from how your body and brain are configured in those moments.

What’s preventing you from consistently doing the work you already know is important?

The state you’re in on the “other days.”

03

Gone in 60 Seconds

How a few seconds can decide your whole week.

  1. Monday, 9 AM

    You’re coming off a nice weekend with friends or family and feel good about getting into the work. You sit down to do outreach, look at your list, and remember how slow Thursday and Friday were. Something shifts in your gut.

  2. 9:05 AM

    You reach for your phone. A few Instagram messages, some reels, your inbox, LinkedIn. It’s noon.

  3. 2 PM

    You’re back from a long lunch. The best time to reach out is gone, you tell yourself, so you leave it for tomorrow and find busy work for the rest of the day.

  4. Tuesday

    Your favorite client comes back asking for new work. You’re so back.

  5. Wed to Fri

    You do all your outreach and close 2 new clients. Life is golden.

  6. Next Monday

    It all starts over. This time there’s no client message on Tuesday, so most of the week stays dull, buried in busy work and reels.

The process

We reverse engineer what’s going on during your “amazing day,” and build practices that rewire the biological state your brain is entering.

So whether the client calls or not, you can have that amazing week, and create it on your own terms.

After weeks and months of this rewiring, when you start work on Monday morning, you can choose your state with much more ease.

05

Lifelong Change

Built where you actually work.

The incomplete solutions mostly failed for the same reason: they happened in a different state and environment from the one you’re usually in.

The 5 to 60 second practices in this program take about 5 minutes a day, and add up to as many as 900 live repetitions, hardwiring the new behavior into the exact environment where you actually need it.

Each time you practice, it gets easier to get back into that state. Many clients feel a difference in the first few days, and within the first weeks find themselves spontaneously entering the “amazing day” state.

When a client goes silent or a call goes poorly, they still feel the pang in their gut. Then they remember it’s just a state, and choose a new one.

After 3 months, you’ll have rewired how your nervous system responds to adversity, using the same process your brain was already using to create the tension in the first place.
