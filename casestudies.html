Eliminated Multi-Day "Ruts" and Achieved a 30X Return on Investment

Ryan  |  Founder, Recruitment Agency

The Trap

Ryan had done everything a driven founder is supposed to do: read the books, worked through the programs, pushed through 75-day challenges and 80-call days. It kept the business moving, but none of it let him actually enjoy it. And when a deal went wrong, his brain would force a hard shift into a Compromised state, costing him two or more days of frustration before he could bounce back.

The Shift

He worried coaching would take the edge off his highs. Instead, we isolated the state he was in when work felt effortless and built micro-practices for his specific trigger moments, so he could shift back into that Baseline quickly rather than losing 48 hours to a rut. His highs stayed; the time between them got shorter.

The ROI

He did the math going in: a single placement would more than pay for the program. He landed several while we worked together, and by reclaiming his lost days he had his best financial year on record, with an ROI 30 to 40x the cost of the coaching.

"I did the math: if I could use this to close just one extra client, the coaching more than paid for itself. It ended up paying for itself dozens of times over before we even finished working together."
