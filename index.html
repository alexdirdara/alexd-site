Frequently Asked Questions

I'm already slammed with work. How much extra time does this take?

About 5 minutes a day. Practices run 5 to 60 seconds, so even doing them up to 10 times a day adds up to roughly five minutes.

The magic comes from volume: up to 900 repetitions over our 3 months together. It's rare for a system to give you that many chances to practice and master a new skill while only asking for 5 minutes a day.

Should I wait until things calm down in my business before starting?

These practices are designed specifically for when things are at their toughest, getting you into a better state within minutes.

If you get it working at your busiest (which is exactly how it's designed to work), you'll be even more effective when things calm down. As long as you can take 30 seconds now and then, you have everything you need to master this.

I'm already making good money. Is this really worth the investment?

It depends on your goals. If you can clearly see the benefit of snapping back from triggers and difficult moments in seconds or minutes, rather than losing hours or days to them, then it will be worth it.

It means spending more of your days feeling like your best days. It means no longer being at the whim of emotional rollercoasters, and taking back control.

I've bought courses and hired coaches before, and nothing stuck. What if I fail again?

A few things make this system different:

Volume. 900 repetitions in 3 months for just 5 minutes a day means you'll log dozens of reps within your first few days.

Range. You'll have practices to use in the middle of calls, and others to get you moving after a slow period.

Feedback you can feel. Your body and mind will feel the benefits within the first couple of days. You don't have to guess whether it's working; you'll feel it, again and again.

And worst case, if you truly feel it isn't doing anything for you, we speak openly and figure out the way forward together.

I often forget to do practices. What if I start the coaching and don't follow through?

This is exactly why the first phase, Awareness, matters so much. Most practices are chosen arbitrarily, so they rarely feel worth doing.

Once we understand you and your patterns, your practices are personalized to how you operate, what you enjoy doing, and what you need. They're designed to create quick shifts in the moment, so your brain immediately sees the benefit and wants more of it.

If some practices feel exciting at first but get dull, we'll find new ones that feel exciting again. I want our time together to be not only productive, but something you're excited to go through.

What's the science behind this methodology?

Recent breakthroughs in state-based learning: the neuroscience showing that we learn and operate in states. We're in one state at work, another in the therapist's office, and another at dinner with the kids.

Expecting learning in one state to transfer to another is why most methods fail. It's why Thursday's therapy doesn't carry into Monday's difficult call.
