Questions founders & executives ask before they start

Who is this for?

Founders and executives who are building something significant and have a clear vision of how they want their business, career or lifestyle to look.

What if I don’t have the time for coaching right now?

That’s the most common reason founders and executives wait, and usually the clearest sign this is for them.

If you’re always busy but no closer to the vision you started with, that’s the thing we’re here to fix.

How is this any different from other coaching I’ve tried?

Traditional coaching uses frameworks, personality tests and charts to box you into a process. The only framework here is you: how you think, how you see the world, and how you make an impact.

The best way to judge is to experience it for yourself on a free call. This is a free coaching call, not a sales call. You simply decide if you want to continue at the end of it.

What do I get from the free call?

I believe coaching has the capacity to transform the way you think about yourself, your business, your goals and your challenges.

The free coaching call is meant to give you that experience, whether or not you decide to keep going after. The goal is for you to return to your workday better off than before the call.

I don’t believe in sales calls or pitching. We’re simply finding out if it’s a fit for you.
